# GT-DARE Access Help

Connect to GT-DARE, create your account and resolve sign-in problems.

## Connect to GT-DARE

When off campus, connect using the GlobalProtect app before opening
[GT-DARE](https://gt-dare.pace.gatech.edu:5173/). Need the app? Download it from the
[Georgia Tech VPN portal](https://vpn.gatech.edu/). Opening the portal alone does
not connect your computer. If the site still does not open after connecting,
contact GT-OSPO for help.

The home address opens the login page. If you are already signed in, it opens
your dashboard, or onboarding if you have not completed it.

## Choose how to sign up

Use one path for each email address. Requesting an invitation and registering
without a code are different paths; submitting both can create a duplicate-request
message. If you already have a request or account, contact GT-OSPO rather than
submitting another request.

### Register without an invitation code

1. From login, choose **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name and email address. Choose and confirm your own password, using at least 8 characters.
3. Leave **Access Code (optional)** blank and choose **Create Account**.
4. Open the verification email and follow its link. Keep the VPN connected.
5. Wait for an administrator to approve your signup. You will receive an email
   about the decision and an account-ready message once both steps are complete.
6. Return to login and sign in with the email and password you chose.

Email verification and administrator approval can happen in either order. Both
are required before a code-free account can sign in. An approval email may still
ask you to verify your email; approval alone does not finish that step.

### Register with an invitation code

1. If you already have an invitation, choose **Sign up**.
2. Use the same email address the invitation was sent to.
3. Choose your own password, enter the invitation code and choose **Create Account**.
4. Follow the verification link sent to that email address, then sign in.

An invitation issued through Request Access is tied to your email, expires and
can be used once. Its approval replaces the separate signup-review step; email
verification is still required. Do not share the code or verification link.

### Request an invitation first

1. On login, choose **Request an invitation**.
2. Enter your name and email. You can include a short reason for needing access.
3. Submit the request and wait for the administrator's decision.
4. If approved, use the code in the invitation email to register as described above.

Request Access does not create an account or password. You choose your password
later, when registering with the invitation.

## Understand your account status

| What you see or receive | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Email verification needed | Open the verification email and follow its link. |
| Email verified; approval pending | Wait for the administrator's decision. Verification alone does not activate a code-free account. |
| Signup approved; verification still needed | Follow your verification link before signing in. |
| Account ready | Sign in with your registered email and password. |
| Request declined | Read the notification and contact GT-OSPO if you have questions. A declined request does not grant access. |
| Account inactive | Contact GT-OSPO. Approval may have been reversed, or the account may have been disabled separately. |

Administrators can revise a decision. DARE sends a notification when the decision
changes. Reversing approval can revoke account access and invalidate an unused
invitation. Reapproval does not remove the email-verification requirement.

## Complete onboarding

After your first successful sign-in, fill in the onboarding form about your role,
field, intended use and how you heard about DARE. Choose **Complete Onboarding**.
You will then reach the dashboard. These profile answers do not grant administrator
permissions or change your access role.

After onboarding, use **User Manual** in the sidebar for instructions on conversations, sources and other tools.

## Reset your password

1. On login, choose **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered email and submit the reset request.
3. Open the newest password-reset email while connected to the GT network or VPN.
4. Choose and confirm your new password on the reset form, then return to login.

Resetting a password does not approve a pending account or reactivate a declined
or disabled account. If a link has expired or has already been used, request a new
one. Never send your password to an administrator or include it in a support email.

## Resolve common problems

- **The site does not open:** check your VPN connection, then try [opening GT-DARE](https://gt-dare.pace.gatech.edu:5173/) again.
  If it still fails, contact GT-OSPO to confirm network access.
- **No verification or invitation email:** check spam/junk and the address you
  entered. Use the verification **Resend** control where offered. Contact GT-OSPO
  if it still does not arrive; do not repeatedly register.
- **A link does not open:** reconnect to the VPN and use the original DARE link in
  the latest email. Ask for help if it opens an error page.
- **A code is rejected:** use the invited email address and copy the whole code.
  An expired, used or revoked invitation needs administrator review.
- **An access request already exists:** do not submit another signup path for
  the same email. Ask GT-OSPO to check the existing request.
- **Too many requests (429):** wait before retrying. Repeated submissions
  are unlikely to help. Contact GT-OSPO if the message persists.
- **Verified but cannot sign in:** check whether approval is still pending.
  Password resets do not bypass approval.

## Contact GT-OSPO

Email [gt-ospo@gatech.edu](mailto:gt-ospo@gatech.edu), or reply to a GT-DARE account
notification. The current sender appears as **GT OSPO** using
**no-reply@pace.gatech.edu**; replies are directed to **gt-ospo@gatech.edu**.

Include your account email, the action you attempted, the time and the exact error.
A screenshot can help, but hide passwords, invitation codes, verification/reset
links and other private information before sharing it.
